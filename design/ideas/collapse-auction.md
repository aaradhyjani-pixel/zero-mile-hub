# The collapse auction (idea #5, design/fun.md)

```
TITLE:          A collapsed airline's slots, tonight
MODE:           PAPER (the auction), then LIVE (the evening bank with the new flights)
CHAPTER, WHEN:  Turbulence · 2046, the evening after an airline stops flying
CAUSE:          an airline has stopped flying (the "anchor collapse" crisis, if you backed one airline; or a
                smaller carrier otherwise). Its slots at VSI and the jets parked on your stands are up for grabs.
                What you can do depends on your cash and whether you cured the crisis with your own money (the
                banks' letter). Paper pin: "Letter from the banks, 2046."
ON SCREEN:      a sealed-bid sheet on the desk: "Evening bank, 18:00–21:00: 14 slots. Four airlines bidding."
                Beside it, a pencil list of the jets parked on your pier, with their unpaid fees.
CHOICES (≤3):   1. Back IndiGo or Akasa Air to take the whole evening bank: you give them a year's discount
                   on charges; the bank refills next chapter.
                2. Split it between two airlines: slower to refill, no discount, less risk if one fails.
                3. Hold the slots yourself for a new long-haul partner: empty evenings now, a bigger bank later
                   if one comes (the A380 pitch, #11, can follow).
                Separately, the parked jets: tow them to the remote apron (cost now) or leave them (your pier
                stays blocked until the lessors collect them).
EFFECT NOW:     the evening shift that follows plays live with the bank you chose: full, half or empty, with
                the parked jets where you left them.
ECHO:           Landing's front page; airline trust; a Black Box bar ("the collapse auction").
TRUTH:          Jet Airways stopped flying in April 2019 and Go First in May 2023; their airport slots were
                temporarily given to other airlines (sources to add to research/). The auction format, the
                discount and the refill speed are game estimates (est.).
SCORE:          Wow 3 / Agency 3 / Link 2 / Cost M → 4.0
```
