# The election inauguration (idea #8, design/fun.md)

```
TITLE:          Open it before the election
MODE:           PAPER (the request), then LIVE (opening day, if you open early)
CHAPTER, WHEN:  Landing · 2059, phase 2 (the satellite piers) is about 85% built
CAUSE:          you committed phase 2 to a date at Landing's board. State officials want it opened before the
                election's model code of conduct starts in six weeks, when new projects can no longer be
                inaugurated. Paper pin: "Board minutes, 2059: phase 2 committed."
ON SCREEN:      a letter on official paper: "The Honourable Chief Minister would be pleased to inaugurate the new
                piers on 14 March." Pencilled beside it by your project director: "Baggage system 70% tested.
                Two of six jet bridges not certified."
CHOICES (≤3):   1. Open early, for the ceremony: the next shift is a LIVE opening day with half the bridges
                   and the new belts untested (incidents likely), and the State's goodwill.
                2. Open on the safe date, after the election: no ceremony, no goodwill bonus, a clean opening.
                3. Hold a ceremony with no passengers (the ribbon, but no flights until it's ready): a smaller
                   goodwill bonus, and the front page notices.
EFFECT NOW:     goodwill moves the State's next decisions (the land for Zero Mile District, the metro link);
                an early opening adds risk to the next shift.
ECHO:           the front page ("PIERS OPEN ON TIME FOR THE CM" or "BAGS PILE UP AT NEW PIERS"); a Black Box line.
TRUTH:          India's Election Commission's model code of conduct restricts announcing and inaugurating new
                projects once an election is announced (source to add to research/). The officials, dates and
                goodwill effects are game estimates (est.). No real politician is named or quoted.
SCORE:          Wow 3 / Agency 3 / Link 2 / Cost M → 4.0
```
