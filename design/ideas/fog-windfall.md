# Fog windfall (idea #1, design/fun.md)

```
TITLE:          The north lands at Nagpur
MODE:           LIVE: the player steers the airport sim
CHAPTER, WHEN:  Climb · a December morning shift, 2034, 04:00
CAUSE:          Delhi, Lucknow and Jaipur are fogged in. How big the windfall is depends on what you built:
                - remote and widebody stands (Pushback's phase-1 size, any stands bought at a board): each free
                  stand is one more diverted jet you can take;
                - the fog landing system on one of your runways (if bought): without it, VSI is fogged too at
                  06:00 and the window closes after two hours;
                - airline trust (Akasa Air's trust from Takeoff, the hold-London record): high trust means
                  airlines ask you first.
                Paper pin on the first diverted jet: "Phase 1 plan, 2026: 14 remote stands."
ON SCREEN:      04:00, the night map. A radio call: "Delhi is below minimums. Sixty northbound flights are
                looking for an alternate. Can Nagpur take them?" The incident ring sits on the remote apron, and
                inbound tracks bend south towards VSI one by one.
GOAL (one line): Park every diverted jet you accept, and keep the 06:00 morning bank on time.
WHAT THE PLAYER DOES (live levers, not buttons):
                - the runway desk: more arrivals now, and swap back before the morning bank;
                - stands: where each diverted jet parks; full stands mean holding aircraft;
                - one call at 04:05 (3 options): take all who ask; take only as many as you have free stands;
                  or refuse and protect the morning bank. Every diverted jet in the air is a fuel call,
                  which is safety and never priced: it lands whatever you chose.
RESULT:         - money: landing and parking fees, plus passengers stranded for hours spending in the terminal;
                - fame: "THE NORTH LANDS AT NAGPUR" or "NAGPUR TURNS AWAY THE FOG";
                - trust: airlines remember who took them. A share of the diverted airlines add a Nagpur route
                  next chapter.
ECHO:           Cruise's front page; a Black Box bar ("the fog night"); a Thiel line under Durability if trust rose.
TRUTH:          Delhi's winter fog regularly forces diversions (CAT III operations exist because of it; source
                to add to research/). The size of this night, the fees and the route offers are game
                estimates (est.).
SCORE:          Wow 3 / Agency 3 / Link 2 / Cost M → 4.0
```
