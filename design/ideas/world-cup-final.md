# World Cup final at Nagpur's cricket stadium (idea #10, design/fun.md)

```
TITLE:          The final comes to Nagpur
MODE:           LIVE: the player steers the airport sim
CHAPTER, WHEN:  Landing · a set piece inside the evening shift, 2059 (or Cruise's era, if Landing's shift isn't
                live yet)
CAUSE:          the cricket board picks Nagpur's stadium for a World Cup final. How hard the day is depends on
                what you own: the number of widebody stands (phase 1 lean or big, phase 2), the remote apron,
                the access road (Zero Mile District adds traffic, buses help) and the airlines' trust.
ON SCREEN:      the Zero Mile Times morning edition: "THE FINAL COMES TO NAGPUR: 90 CHARTERS, ONE EVENING".
                Then the map. Charter flights stream in from Mumbai, Delhi, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Dubai and
                London, all landing between 12:00 and 18:00, on top of the normal day. A gold "match" banner
                shows the toss time and a countdown.
GOAL (one line): Land the charters before the first ball at 14:00 and get them all out by 01:00, without
                breaking the normal evening bank.
WHAT THE PLAYER DOES (it's the live sim, so these are the levers, not buttons):
                - the runway desk: more arrivals before the toss, more departures after the last ball;
                - stands: park charters on the remote apron with buses, or on the cargo apron (if built);
                - two calls during the slice (at most 3 options each; safety never priced):
                  1. 13:10 · "Both teams' charters are 20 minutes late, together." Hold a runway for them
                     (normal arrivals stack up), or land them in sequence (the team bus misses the warm-up;
                     the front page notices).
                  2. 22:40 · "The match goes to a super over. 60 charters will all want to leave at once."
                     Open a fourth departure runway (arrivals divert), or meter departures (charters wait
                     on stands until 02:00; airlines claim penalties).
RESULT:         stars, charters on time, normal flights kept on time, fans who missed the toss.
                - Money: charter landing and parking fees plus fans' spending in the terminal (est.), less
                  penalties.
                - Fame: the next front page reads "NAGPUR HANDLED THE FINAL" or "FANS MISS THE TOSS".
                - Trust: the airlines' trust rises or falls, and that moves the next route offers.
ECHO:           the front page above; one Black Box papers-strip entry; a Mastery line if it was 3★.
TRUTH:          Nagpur's international cricket stadium is real and has hosted international matches; the
                final, the charter numbers and the fees are game estimates (est.).
SCORE:          Wow 3 / Agency 3 / Link 2 / Cost M → 4.0
BUILD:          reuse Level 4's set-piece machinery (the summit's live pacing, banner and radio calls) with a
                charter schedule layered on the shift's own; no new 3D.
```
