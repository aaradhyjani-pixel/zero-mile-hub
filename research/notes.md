- RECAT-EU category assignments relevant to a widebody fleet: B77W, B789, B788, A359, A333 and B748 are CAT-B Upper Heavy; B763, B752 and MD11 are CAT-C Lower Heavy; A319, A320, A321 and B738 are CAT-D Upper Medium; ATR72, E190 and CRJ9 are CAT-E Lower Medium; the A380 (A388) is CAT-A. (https://code7700.com/pdfs/wake_turbulence_recat-eu-september-2015.pdf)
- At Atlanta, the FAA reports that reduced 2.5 NM arrival separation is not available in low instrument conditions, so low-IMC capacity drops to 182 to 186 operations per hour in East flow and 177 to 178 in West flow. The IMC West-flow range is 197 to 204. (https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/airports/planning_capacity/profiles/ATL-Airport-Capacity-Profile-2018.pdf)

A330-200 (513,675 lb): 10,700 ft at ISA and 11,400 ft at ISA+15. (https://www.lawa.org/media/30449)
